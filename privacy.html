Privacy policy

Personal Assistant is a personal app. Only its owner uses it, on the owner's own computer.

  • The app reads Gmail and Google Calendar data only for the owner, after the owner signs in.
  • All data stays on the owner's computer. The app has no server and no other users.
  • The app does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone.
  • The app does not use Google user data for advertising or to train AI models.
  • The owner can remove the app's access at any time in their Google Account settings.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
